Every F1 car already reports where it is. This HUD turns that into a navigation radar that sees around the next corners and through spray. Drive it yourself, race other cars wheel to wheel, drop a hazard behind a blind corner, or load your own circuit from a map.

Phone wheel

Scan the code with your Android phone's camera and hold the phone sideways like a steering wheel. Tilt it to steer. The two big buttons on the phone are brake and gas.
